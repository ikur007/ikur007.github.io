2026 Guide to Sustainable Coffee Sourcing

Coffee sourcing practices have shifted significantly over the past few years, with more roasters prioritizing direct trade relationships with growers. This shift reflects growing consumer demand for transparency in the supply chain, from farm to cup.

Certification programs remain a useful signal for consumers, though critics note that certification alone does not guarantee fair compensation for farmers. Direct trade models attempt to address this gap by removing intermediaries and establishing longer-term purchasing agreements.
